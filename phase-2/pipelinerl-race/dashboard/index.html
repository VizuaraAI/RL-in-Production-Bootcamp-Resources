How to read this

GPU busy % is the honest systems number: what fraction of the GPU-seconds you paid for were actually doing work. Samples/s is throughput. Mean reward is effectiveness. Learning speed is roughly the product — a run that wins on throughput but loses badly on reward has not won.

Mean lag only applies to PipelineRL: how many optimizer steps stale the training data was. Near zero means your generators are barely ahead of your trainers (you could give generation more GPUs); a large value means the split is lopsided the other way.

A cpu transport or not in-flight badge means that run did not use the real PipelineRL weight path, so its transfer cost is inflated and its numbers are not comparable to runs that did. That is worth saying out loud rather than quietly averaging in.
