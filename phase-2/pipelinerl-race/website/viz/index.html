Drop a run's events.jsonl here (or two, one per arm) to replay a real run. Nothing is uploaded — it is read in your browser.

How to read this. In the conventional arm all GPUs share the same job: they generate together, then they all stop and train together. Watch the batch drain near the end of each generation phase — sequences finish at different times, so GPUs go pale while the cluster waits for the slowest one. In PipelineRL the GPUs are split: the ochre ones never stop generating and the red ones never stop training, and the slate pulses are weights moving between them without generation pausing. The idle GPU-seconds counter is the honest scoreboard — it is compute you paid for and did not use.
